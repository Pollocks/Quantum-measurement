Units: the well width is L = 1 and the ground-state energy is E1 = 1. Thus En = n2, and time is measured in ℏ/E1.
Position measurement: the result is sampled from the pre-measurement |ψ(x,t)|2. The post-measurement state is a Gaussian-like packet multiplied by sin(πx/L), so it is exactly zero at the walls. Δx controls its approximate width. The localized state is projected onto 40 box eigenstates.
Energy measurement: the result is sampled from the current eigenstate probabilities and collapses the system to that box eigenstate. Its |ψ|2 remains stationary while the clock continues to advance.
Initial state: Reset prepares a normalized superposition of the selected number of low-lying eigenstates. With 2 states, the default is the equal-weight, phase-aligned state (|1⟩+|2⟩)/√2.
The finite 40-state basis is a deliberate pedagogical approximation, especially after a narrow position measurement.
